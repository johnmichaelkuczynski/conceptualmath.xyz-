Developmental Mathematics Demo Video

See how Developmental Mathematics rebuilds arithmetic from place value through fractions, percents, ratios, and the start of algebra. This animated demo follows a learner through lectures, an AI tutor, adaptive practice, analytics, and assignment feedback.

What the video shows

  1. Course dashboard: a four-week learning plan begins with whole numbers and operations.
  2. Place value lecture: a learner reads about why each digit is worth ten times the place to its right, then switches to a longer explanation.
  3. AI tutor: the learner asks why division by zero is undefined and receives a grounded explanation.
  4. Analytics: course progress and topic mastery make practice needs visible, including ratios and proportions.
  5. Adaptive practice: a prime-factorization problem responds to the learner's attempt and adjusts difficulty.
  6. Assignment feedback: a graded homework review combines the learner's answer with explanatory AI feedback.
