**Rule:** Developmental Mathematics must remain completely open to learners: no login, logout, account requirement, guest tier, or AI-text allowance. Every learner-facing course feature and diagnostic assessment is intentionally available without an account.

**Why:** The owner explicitly required removal of all login and all functionality limits while preserving diagnostics, analytics, assessments, gradebook, visitor counting, and AI features.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce learner access gates or usage caps unless the owner explicitly reverses this policy.
